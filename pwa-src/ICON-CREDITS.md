# App icon credits

The SolidCutlist app icon (`pwa/icons/*.png`) is the **"wood" (🪵) emoji from Google's
Noto Emoji** (`icon-log.svg` here is the original vector), placed on the app's dark colour
`#212529`.

- Source: https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji (via the `@iconify-json/noto` package)
- License: **Apache License 2.0**: https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji/blob/main/svg/LICENSE
  Free for commercial use; keep this attribution with the project.

The icons were rendered from the SVG at 1024 px and scaled to 512/192/180/32 px
(maskable version with a larger safe-zone margin).
